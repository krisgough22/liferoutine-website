Habit at a Time. Always.

Most self-help books for young adults are full of inspiration but short on specifics. Life Routine is different. It gives you a straightforward, no-fluff framework for building daily habits across the eight areas of life that actually matter.

Using the Life Routine Wheel as your map, each chapter tackles one area head-on: what it means, why it matters for young adults specifically, and exactly what you can start doing tomorrow to improve it.

This book is for young adults who feel like they're grinding but not growing. People who are ambitious but scattered. Anyone who knows what they should be doing but can't seem to build the habits to actually do it.

Success isn't one big breakthrough. It's the accumulation of small, intentional daily actions across every important area of your life. Take the first step—start building your Life Routine today.

The Life Routine Wheel

Eight Areas.
One System.

01 — Foundation
Health
Sleep, movement, nutrition, and energy management. The engine on which everything else runs. When it's neglected, every other area costs more.
02 — Career
Professional
Career clarity, daily work habits, skill-building, and knowing the difference between being busy and actually moving forward.
03 — Money
Finance
Budgeting, savings routines, debt management, and the money mindset that separates people who build security from those who feel perpetually behind.
04 — Environment
Household
Systems that make your home a restorative, functional environment rather than a source of low-grade daily friction and stress.
05 — Community
Social / Friendships
Intentional maintenance of real friendships in an era where it's never been easier to feel connected and never more common to feel genuinely alone.
06 — Connection
Love & Family
Your most intimate relationships — romantic and familial — and the daily habits that keep them honest, healthy, and genuinely sustaining.
07 — Expression
Creativity
The practice of making things and maintaining the playful, generative part of yourself that most people slowly abandon in the name of being a responsible adult.
08 — Inner Life
Spirituality
Mindfulness, values, purpose, and the inner life practices that keep you grounded in who you are and what matters — regardless of religious background.

About the Author

Kristen M. Gough
Author · Life Routine

Kristen M. Gough wrote Life Routine for the person she once was — ambitious, motivated in bursts, but unable to make the pieces stick together consistently. She understands the gap between knowing what you should do and actually building the habits to do it.

Her approach is direct, practical, and grounded in the belief that structure is not the opposite of freedom — it is the precondition for it. Life Routine is the book she wished had existed when she was 22, figuring it all out.
